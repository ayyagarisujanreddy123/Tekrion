# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Tekrion is an open-source project for investigating AI-agent behavior. We are committed to making participation respectful, safe and useful for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- State uncertainty honestly and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy, consent and the sensitivity of recorded evidence.
- Help make project spaces accessible to newcomers and experienced contributors alike.
- Accept reasonable moderation decisions and disengage when asked.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling, personal attacks, sustained disruption or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Posting credentials, private source, captured evidence or other sensitive material to make a point.
- Impersonation or knowingly misleading claims about project findings, security or maintainers.

## Scope

This Code applies in repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, community events and public spaces where someone is representing Tekrion.

## Reporting and enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, issue warnings or restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this Code.

For an ordinary moderation concern, open an issue containing only the minimum non-sensitive context needed. For a confidential report, use the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/ayyagarisujanreddy123/Tekrion/security/advisories/new), begin the title with `[Conduct]` and do not include third-party secrets or private evidence. Platform-level abuse may also be reported directly to GitHub.

Maintainers will review reports as promptly and impartially as practical, protect reporter privacy where possible and disclose conflicts of interest.
